An Evil Twin is a rogue wireless access point configured to broadcast the exact SSID of a trusted network (e.g. airport or university WiFi) to capture victim credentials. Our Evil Twin Simulator checks for BSSID (MAC Address) mismatches and encryption protocol shifts.
Safe Browsing Guidelines
  • Verify Domain Spelling Always double-check address bar spellings for homograph tricks (e.g. paypaI.com vs paypal.com).
  • Ensure Valid HTTPS & SSL Never transmit passwords or financial data over plain HTTP connections.
  • Use Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) Enable 2FA across all critical personal and corporate accounts.
