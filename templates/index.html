Understanding Phishing Attacks

Phishing is a social engineering cyber attack where malicious actors create deceptive websites that closely resemble trusted platforms such as banks, email providers, or payment gateways.

The goal of an attacker is to trick unsuspecting users into entering confidential credentials, passwords, credit card numbers, or personal identifying information.

Deceptive Domain Names

Attackers substitute letters or add extra hyphens (e.g., paypaI.com with an uppercase 'I' or login-bank-secure.xyz).

Unencrypted HTTP Connections

Phishing sites often lack valid SSL/TLS certificates or utilize suspicious non-standard top-level domains.

Urgency & Phishing Keywords

URLs frequently embed keywords like verify, wallet, update, or signin to trick victims into rapid action.
